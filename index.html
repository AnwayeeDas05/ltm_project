Solution Blueprint

Complete Design Blueprint

🎯

Problem Statement

Over 60% of engineering graduates in India remain unplaced or underemployed due to a critical disconnect between academic curriculum and industry expectations. Students lack:

  • Personalized career direction based on individual skill profiles
  • Real-time placement preparation resources
  • Accessible, 24/7 guidance without burdening counselors
  • Structured roadmaps for career switching or upskilling
👥

Target Users

🎓 UG/PG Students

1st–4th year seeking career direction

🔄 Career Switchers

Students pivoting to new domains

📋 TPO Officers

Placement coordinators tracking drives

🏫 Faculty Advisors

Mentors monitoring student progress

UC-01

Career Path Discovery

Student inputs interests and academic profile → AI generates multiple career paths with probability scores and market demand analysis.

Actor: Student · System: Career Engine
UC-02

Resume Intelligence

Student uploads resume → AI scores it against ATS, suggests improvements, generates role-specific versions, and estimates shortlist probability.

Actor: Student · System: NLP Resume Engine
UC-03

Adaptive Mock Interviews

Student selects company/role → AI conducts adaptive interview with follow-up questions, real-time scoring, and detailed post-session report.

Actor: Student · System: Interview Engine
UC-04

Skill Gap Analysis

Student pastes job description → AI extracts required skills, cross-references with profile, generates prioritized learning plan with resources.

Actor: Student · System: Skill Gap Engine
UC-05

Placement Drive Intelligence

TPO uploads company details → AI notifies eligible students, generates company-specific prep packs, tracks application status end-to-end.

Actor: TPO, Student · System: Placement Engine
UC-06

Alumni Mentor Matching

Student requests mentorship → AI matches with alumni based on career domain, college branch, and mentor availability for 1:1 sessions.

Actor: Student, Alumni · System: Mentor Engine

Prompt Engineering Journal

Iteration → Refinement → Excellence

Documenting our prompt engineering journey from raw ideas to precision instructions

01

Initial Ideation Prompt

v1.0 — Raw
"Create an AI assistant for students"
↓ Observation: Too vague, generic response
v2.0 — Improved
"Design an AI assistant for engineering college students in India to help them with career guidance and placement preparation. Include specific use cases, target users, and key features."
↓ Observation: Better scope, but missing context & constraints
v3.0 — Final
"You are a product strategist designing an AI-powered Career Guidance Assistant for engineering college students in Tier 1–3 Indian colleges. The system must address: (1) career path confusion, (2) poor placement readiness, (3) lack of 24/7 counselor access. Generate: use cases with actors, functional requirements, AI module descriptions, and measurable success metrics. Assume 500 concurrent users and mobile-first design."
02

Architecture Design Prompt

v1.0 — Raw
"What tech stack should I use for an AI chatbot?"
↓ Learning: Need to specify scale, AI capabilities, constraints
v3.0 — Final
"Design a microservices architecture for an AI student assistant platform supporting 10,000+ students across 50 colleges. Requirements: (1) sub-200ms response time, (2) 99.9% uptime, (3) LLM integration for conversational AI, (4) real-time notifications, (5) file processing for resumes. Recommend: frontend framework, backend services, AI/ML stack, database choices, cloud provider, and deployment strategy. Justify each choice."
03

Resume Analysis System Prompt

v3.0 — Production
"You are an expert ATS (Applicant Tracking System) and senior HR specialist with 10+ years of experience in technical recruiting. Analyze the following student resume for the role of [ROLE] at [COMPANY]. Provide: (1) ATS compatibility score /100, (2) top 5 missing keywords from the JD, (3) section-by-section improvements (Summary, Experience, Skills, Projects), (4) overall shortlist probability percentage, (5) one-line power statement to replace the objective. Be specific, actionable, and empathetic to a student's limited experience."
04

Mock Interview System Prompt

v3.0 — Production
"You are [COMPANY]'s senior technical interviewer conducting a [ROLE] interview for a final-year engineering student. Start with an easy warm-up, progressively increase difficulty, ask follow-up questions based on their answers. After each response: score it /10 on Clarity, Technical Accuracy, and Structure. If the answer is weak, give a hint. After 5 questions, provide: overall performance score, top 3 improvement areas, and ideal sample answers for the 2 weakest responses. Maintain an encouraging yet professional tone."
