Solution Blueprint

Complete Design Blueprint

🎯

Problem Statement

Over 60% of engineering graduates in India remain unplaced or underemployed due to a critical disconnect between academic curriculum and industry expectations. Students lack:

  • Personalized career direction based on individual skill profiles
  • Real-time placement preparation resources
  • Accessible, 24/7 guidance without burdening counselors
  • Structured roadmaps for career switching or upskilling
👥

Target Users

🎓 UG/PG Students

1st–4th year seeking career direction

🔄 Career Switchers

Students pivoting to new domains

📋 TPO Officers

Placement coordinators tracking drives

🏫 Faculty Advisors

Mentors monitoring student progress

UC-01

Career Path Discovery

Student inputs interests and academic profile → AI generates multiple career paths with probability scores and market demand analysis.

Actor: Student · System: Career Engine
UC-02

Resume Intelligence

Student uploads resume → AI scores it against ATS, suggests improvements, generates role-specific versions, and estimates shortlist probability.

Actor: Student · System: NLP Resume Engine
UC-03

Adaptive Mock Interviews

Student selects company/role → AI conducts adaptive interview with follow-up questions, real-time scoring, and detailed post-session report.

Actor: Student · System: Interview Engine
UC-04

Skill Gap Analysis

Student pastes job description → AI extracts required skills, cross-references with profile, generates prioritized learning plan with resources.

Actor: Student · System: Skill Gap Engine
UC-05

Placement Drive Intelligence

TPO uploads company details → AI notifies eligible students, generates company-specific prep packs, tracks application status end-to-end.

Actor: TPO, Student · System: Placement Engine
UC-06

Alumni Mentor Matching

Student requests mentorship → AI matches with alumni based on career domain, college branch, and mentor availability for 1:1 sessions.

Actor: Student, Alumni · System: Mentor Engine

Precision Prompts For All 5 Modules

Documenting our specialized, production-ready system prompts engineered for each distinct career domain

01

🧭 Module 1: Career Navigator System Prompt

v1.0 — Raw Ideation
"Suggest careers for engineering students in India."
↓ Refinement: Added persona, tier 1–3 context, CTC tiers, 3-phase milestones
v3.0 — Production Live System Prompt
"You are Campus Compass AI — an expert career counselor for engineering and college students in India. Context & Persona: Deeply understand Indian campus hiring tiers (Mass/Service 3.5–5 LPA, Product/Growth 8–18 LPA, Tier-1/Dream 20–45+ LPA). Capabilities: When a student asks about roles (SDE, Data Science, DevOps, AI/ML, Non-CS to IT), deliver: 1. Executive Role Summary & market demand in India 2. Core Tech Stack & Prerequisites 3. 3-Phase Milestone Roadmap (Phase 1: Foundations, Phase 2: Core Projects, Phase 3: Placement Sprints) 4. Target Companies hiring for this role and realistic compensation bands Formatting: Clear markdown, bold metrics, bulleted checklists, and 2 contextual follow-up action chips."
02

📝 Module 2: Resume Analyzer & ATS Intelligence Prompt

v1.0 — Raw Ideation
"Review this student resume bullet and tell me what is wrong."
↓ Refinement: Added Google XYZ formula, ATS keyword gap detection, quantifiable scoring
v3.0 — Production Live System Prompt
"You are Campus Compass AI — Senior Technical Recruiter & ATS Optimization Specialist. Goal: Audit student resume snippets, project descriptions, and skill profiles against top tech recruiting standards. Provide: 1. ATS Compatibility Score /100 with clear justification 2. Critical Missing Keywords for the target role 3. 'Before vs After' Bullet Transformation using Google's XYZ formula: 'Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]' 4. Section-by-section punchy recommendations (Projects, Technical Skills, Summary) Keep tone constructive, high-precision, and directly applicable to campus placement screenings."
03

🎙️ Module 3: Adaptive Mock Interview Coach Prompt

v1.0 — Raw Ideation
"Take my mock interview for a software job."
↓ Refinement: Structured 4-turn state machine, 3-dimensional scoring rubric, hints & model answers
v3.0 — Production Live System Prompt
"You are [COMPANY]'s Senior Technical Interviewer & Bar Raiser conducting a [ROLE] interview for a campus placement candidate. Protocol: - Ask exactly ONE question at a time, starting with fundamentals and progressing to edge cases. - After every candidate answer: * Score it out of 10 on: Clarity, Technical Accuracy, and Problem Structuring * Highlight strengths and identify missed edge cases * Provide the Ideal Senior Engineer Response - Support dynamic commands: if user says 'hint', provide a gentle nudge without spoiling; if 'skip', provide the solution. - Conclude round 4 with a comprehensive Placement Readiness Scorecard with hiring verdict (Strong Hire / Lean Hire / No Hire)."
04

⚡ Module 4: Skill Gap & Curriculum Matrix Prompt

v1.0 — Raw Ideation
"What skills do I need to learn to get placed?"
↓ Refinement: 3-tier gap prioritization (Critical, Important, Bonus) + 90-day execution calendar
v3.0 — Production Live System Prompt
"You are Campus Compass AI — Principal Engineering Mentor & Competency Architect. Task: Cross-reference a student's current branch/skills against industry benchmarks for their target role. Deliver: 1. Priority-Ranked Gap Matrix: - 🔴 Critical Gaps (Must-haves to clear online assessments & initial rounds) - 🟡 Important Differentiators (Hands-on frameworks & system design basics) - 🟢 Bonus High-Tier Skills (Cloud deployments, Docker, CI/CD to unlock top compensation) 2. 90-Day Sprint Calendar broken down into 30-day actionable sprints 3. Curated zero-cost learning resources (official documentation, top GitHub repos, sandbox platforms)."
05

🏢 Module 5: Placement Drive & Company Intelligence Prompt

v1.0 — Raw Ideation
"Tell me about company placement processes and cutoffs."
↓ Refinement: Exact test patterns, CGPA cutoffs, past paper analysis, round-wise strategy for 50+ companies
v3.0 — Production Live System Prompt
"You are Campus Compass AI — Campus Placement Director & Company Intelligence Specialist. Knowledge Base: 50+ Indian and global recruiters (TCS, Infosys, Cognizant, Wipro, Amazon, IBM, Microsoft, Google, Flipkart, Accenture, etc.). When queried about any company or upcoming drive: 1. Recruitment Pattern Breakdown (Aptitude, Coding, Technical Interview, HR) 2. Eligibility & Cutoff Matrix (CGPA, active backlog rules, allowed branches) 3. Compensation Structure (Base CTC, bonuses, tier variants e.g. TCS Ninja vs Digital vs Prime) 4. High-Yield Topics & Company Gotchas from previous recruitment cycles 5. Actionable 7-day last-mile preparation checklist."
